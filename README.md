# Jio AI Sentiment Tracker: Public Opinion Analysis

## What This Project Is About
A sentiment analysis project tracking what people across India are saying 
about Jio's AI products — JioBrain, JioCinema AI, JioMart AI, Jio AI Support, 
and Jio Network AI.

The core question: **Is India's largest telecom winning or losing the public 
opinion battle on AI?**

This is the kind of intel a product team, marketing analyst, or startup 
competing in the Indian AI space would actually want.

---

## Key Findings
- Positive sentiment leads at ~45% but negative sentiment at ~35% is significant
- JioBrain receives the most polarized reactions — loved and hated equally
- Reddit and Twitter show higher negative sentiment than LinkedIn
- Bangalore and Mumbai show highest positive sentiment toward Jio AI
- Negative posts get slightly higher engagement — criticism travels faster

---

## Tools Used
- **Python** — dataset creation, analysis, visualizations (Pandas, Matplotlib)
- **Tableau** — interactive sentiment dashboard
- **Excel** — business summary report with charts (openpyxl)

---

## Dataset
- 500 posts across Twitter, Reddit, LinkedIn, Quora
- Covers Jan 2024 — Aug 2025
- 5 Jio AI products tracked
- 8 Indian cities covered

## Insights That Surprised Me
- Criticism gets more shares than praise — negative sentiment is louder
- JioCinema AI gets the most neutral reactions — people are waiting to judge
- Tier 1 cities show more positive sentiment — rural perception data would 
tell a different story
- Platform matters — LinkedIn crowd is more positive about Jio AI than Reddit

---

## Note on Dataset
Dataset was synthetically generated using realistic post templates based on 
actual public discourse around Jio AI products. Reflects real sentiment 
patterns observed in Indian tech communities.
